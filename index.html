Video Player · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: Video Player works fully offline. It has no Internet permission, no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. Everything the app reads or stores stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Video Player (package name com.sabbir.videoplayer), published by Sabbir (“we”, “us”). It explains what the app accesses, what it stores on your device, and what it does not collect.

1.Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.
  • No account, name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising ID is collected.
  • The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • There are no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2.Access to your videos

  • With your permission, the app reads video files from your device’s media library (Android MediaStore) so it can list, search, play, rename, hide, and delete videos that you choose.
  • On Android 13 and later, this uses READ_MEDIA_VIDEO. On Android 14 and later, the app also supports limited access (“Select photos and videos”) through READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED; in that case only the videos you select are visible to the app.
  • On Android 12 and lower, this uses READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, limited to those versions.
  • The app does not access photos, audio files, documents, or other files — only videos.
  • All playback happens locally on your device.

3.Hidden (private) videos

  • If you choose Hide, the app copies the selected video into its private, app-specific storage (the hidden_videos folder inside the app’s internal storage), then asks the system to delete the original from your gallery. That deletion requires your confirmation in a system dialog.
  • While hidden, the video is not visible to other apps or the system gallery.
  • You can restore a hidden video from inside the app. The app copies it back to Movies/Video Player and deletes the private copy.

Important: hidden videos exist only inside the app’s private storage. If you uninstall the app, clear its data, or lose the device, those hidden videos are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Restore anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

4.PIN protection

  • The hidden-videos PIN is stored only on your device as a salted PBKDF2 hash (with a random salt) in the app’s private preferences. The PIN itself is never stored or transmitted.
  • There is no server, account, or recovery service. If you forget the PIN, the app cannot recover it, and the settings screen requires the current PIN before you can set a new one.

5.Other data stored on your device

  • Playback positions, app settings, and per-video interface state are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences.
  • Temporary copies of videos and notification artwork may be written to the app’s cache directory. Android may clear the cache at any time.
  • This data stays on your device. It is not backed up to Google or any cloud service: the app disables Android backup and device-to-device data transfer (allowBackup="false" and exclusion rules).

6.Permissions and why they are used

Permission / featurePurpose

The app does not request Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or phone permissions.

7.Third-party components

The app uses open-source Flutter packages that run on your device and do not transmit data: video_player, photo_manager, audio_service, permission_handler, wakelock_plus, screen_brightness, volume_controller, and shared_preferences. There are no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

8.Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect personal information at all.

9.Your choices and deletion

  • You can revoke media access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Video Player → Permissions. Without it, the app cannot list or play videos.
  • You can delete videos from within the app. Hidden videos should be restored first if you want to keep them.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all app-local data, including hidden videos, settings, and the PIN. This cannot be undone.
  • Because we do not collect data, there is no server-side data to request or delete.
